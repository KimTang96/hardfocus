Privacy Policy

Hardfocus: App Blocker · Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version: Hardfocus doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no tracking, no analytics and no ads. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your sessions, schedules, settings, progress (hours protected, weekly streaks) and the times you used the escape are stored only on your device. They're never sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Screen Time

Hardfocus uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to block the apps you choose. Apple's system handles your app choices and usage; Hardfocus only receives opaque tokens that it can't read. Hardfocus can't see which apps you use, for how long, or what you do in them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they're created on your iPhone: a weekly summary and, during a free trial, one reminder before it ends. Nothing is sent from a server.

Children

Hardfocus is a personal focus tool and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
