Can I cancel a session?

No, that's the point. On the free plan the only way out is the escape: a 30-second pause, then 5 minutes, and it locks again. With Pro's strict mode there's no way out until the session ends.

Why do some changes only apply tomorrow?

Shorter hours, fewer days, unblocking apps, a shorter pause or a longer escape window all make things looser, so they wait until tomorrow. Tightening applies straight away.

Can I turn it off completely?

Yes: iOS Settings → Screen Time → Hardfocus. No app can prevent that. Hardfocus will tell you protection is off, and your hours are kept.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. There's no account to restore it from. Pro purchases can be restored from Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Pro?

In Hardfocus: Settings → Manage subscription. Or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. During a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.
